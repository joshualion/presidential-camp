# Campaign Manager v1.2.5

- Advanced Reports & AI Intelligence: scoped workforce/coverage summaries, geographic drilldowns, date filters, CSV/PDF exports, and optional server-side AI using encrypted credentials, aggregate-sharing consent, usage limits and audit history. Standard reports work independently of AI; provider charges are separate.
- Campaign Finance: scoped accounts, income/expenditure, budgets, independent payment approvals, private evidence, monthly summaries, statements and audits. Integer amounts, locking and idempotency protect posting. This module records finances and does not initiate bank transfers.
- Campaign Merchandise & Inventory: internal materials/categories, scoped stock, receipts, adjustments, requests, cancellation, distribution, low-stock indicators, CSV and immutable history. Transactions prevent duplicate or overdrawn stock movements.
- SMS: fresh installations require a signed module licence. Previously enabled/configured direct-provider deployments receive a bound legacy migration grant. Existing Termii/BulkSMSNigeria settings and data are preserved; Portal credit resale remains disabled.
- Automatic licensing: the signed package bundles the existing official public verification key; private signing material stays on the Portal. Installer/updater initialize enterprise tables and permissions, preserving customer configuration and registration. Checkout return refreshes verified access automatically; expired licences offer renewal.
- Authorization: installation, HTTPS domain and campaign package are verified independently of user capabilities and geographic scope. Existing Portal prices are preserved. Pending payments and refunds cannot activate access.

Offline use is limited by the earlier of individual licence expiry and signed response validity (at most 24 hours); AI requires fresh online authorization. SMS delivery and AI provider charges are separate from module licence fees.

Upgrade through **Official Updates**. Normal customers do not need SSH, Composer, environment edits or manual RSA configuration. Signed workflow rehearsals cover fresh installation and upgrades from official 1.1.3–1.2.4 packages. Preserve existing application keys, registration and data.

The Portal releases the three new checkouts only after official stable v1.2.5 publication and for installations reporting a compatible version. User permissions must still be assigned by the Super Admin.
