# Campaign Manager v1.2.6

Campaign Finance adds configurable, jurisdiction-scoped permissions and appointments. Finance officers with the appropriate permissions can record income and externally paid expenses with supporting evidence. Authorized users can submit funding proposals for independent review and track approvals, allocations, advances and settlements.

Finance retains the existing paid entitlement. The update adds balanced journals, an audit history, budget reservations, independently reviewed bank statement reconciliation and accounting period close and reopen controls. The updater preserves existing Finance records. Accounts with unreconciled historical balances require review before further posting.

Campaign Payment Settings stores separate test and live credentials for a campaign-owned Paystack account. Test collections, beneficiary mappings, single and bulk test transfers, and provider verification are isolated from campaign balances. A Super Admin can match a verified live settlement to reviewed bank statement evidence using a read-only provider request. Live collections, transfers and automatic ledger posting remain disabled. Merchant approval, compliance review and end-to-end validation are required before live money movement can be enabled.

Super Admins can review Finance access after upgrading through Official Updates. Existing licences remain in effect.
