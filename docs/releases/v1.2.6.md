# Campaign Manager v1.2.6

Campaign Finance adds configurable, jurisdiction-scoped permissions and appointments. Finance officers with the appropriate permissions can record income and externally paid expenses with supporting evidence. Authorized users can submit funding proposals for independent review and track approvals, allocations, advances and settlements.

Finance retains the existing paid entitlement. The update adds balanced journals, an audit history, budget reservations, independently reviewed bank statement reconciliation and accounting period close and reopen controls. The updater preserves existing Finance records. Accounts with unreconciled historical balances require review before further posting.

Campaign Payment Settings stores separate test and live credentials for a campaign-owned Paystack account. Members can select their saved profile bank details for reimbursement. The approved request retains the recipient identity and a protected bank snapshot. A bank detail or jurisdiction change requires a new approval. Test collections, beneficiary mappings, single and bulk test transfers, and provider verification are isolated from campaign balances. A Super Admin can match a verified live settlement to reviewed bank statement evidence using a read-only provider request. Officers can independently review and post an existing single-payment donation from an exact Paystack settlement and bank credit, with separate journal entries for gross income and the provider fee. The workflow records restricted donor case references and checks the reviewed cumulative contribution amount. Batch settlements, refunds and other variances require separate reconciliation. Campaign Manager initiates Paystack collections and transfers in sandbox mode only. Live collection or payout initiation requires a separately validated merchant, compliance approval and a future activation path.

Super Admins can review Finance access after upgrading through Official Updates. Existing licences remain in effect.
