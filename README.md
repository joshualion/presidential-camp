## About Campaign Manager

Campaign Manager is a powerful political campaign management solution designed to handle campaigns at various levels, including presidential, state, and local government elections. Built on top of the Laravel framework, it provides a flexible and scalable platform for managing all aspects of a political campaign, from candidates and polling units to voter segmentation.

### Key Features:
- **Presidential Campaign Management:** Manage nationwide campaigns with tools for tracking voter preferences, candidate profiles, and region-based performance.
- **State Campaign Management:** Specifically designed for governors, senators, representatives, and assembly members with tools to monitor and organize regional campaigns.
- **Local Government Campaign Management:** Target local government campaigns, including chairman elections, with detailed management of wards, polling units, and volunteer tracking.
- **Real-time Polling Data:** Stay updated on voter preferences with real-time data and analytics at national, state, and local levels.
- **Candidate & Voter Segmentation:** Easily manage candidate profiles and organize voters by region, state, or local government for focused campaign strategies.
- **Fundraising & Event Management:** Track fundraising efforts and organize campaign events with detailed performance reports.

## Why Use Campaign Manager?

- **Modular Design:** With standalone packages for presidential, state, and local government campaigns, Campaign Manager allows you to scale based on campaign needs.
- **Built on Laravel:** Leverage Laravel's powerful features such as Eloquent ORM, real-time broadcasting, and robust queue management for a seamless development experience.
- **User-friendly:** The intuitive interface makes it easy for campaign staff to manage operations, even with limited technical expertise.
